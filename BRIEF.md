## The problem
- People with vision problems struggle with new areas because they will not be aware and they might have a hard time adjusting to the new area
- Impact area: Environment
- Why it matters (impact): It will make people with blindness throw trash away in the trash can and not trip on trash.
- Evidence it’s real: A survey we ran in 2015 showed that 95 per cent of blind and partially sighted people have collided with an obstacle in their local neighbourhood over a three month period, and that nearly a third of those were injured (RNIB, 2015). https://media.rnib.org.uk/documents/Seeing_Streets_Differently_report_RNIB_2021.pdf
- What people told me: Person A: " Sometimes I notice a lot of trash around trash cans. I think it will be easy to trip on."
## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by telling blind people issues that might come up in the places they are visiting, which works better than asking someone random, because 1. they might deceive you 2. the AI can look up every location
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: BlindSquare, what makes our's different is that BlindSquare focues on relevant information instead of hazards
- Safety plan: it might say this route is completly safe but it actually has some hazards. Our website will make it so it will say things like, this is AI generated so it may not be completly correct.
## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: The user types a place and it shows them the difficult of the place they are going
- Its job: To deliver info about places to people with blindness
- Who it talks to and how to sound: People with vision difficulties and it should sound nice, not mean
- Facts it must use: Look up the facts online
- Rules it must follow: Be mean to people, go off topic or even if they know the right answer, decive people
- How to answer: It shouldn't be too long, but it should be long enough to provide enough info about the place they are going to as in difficulties
